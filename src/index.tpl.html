You do, outright. Every line of code, every configuration, every workflow transfers to you at the end. No vendor lock-in. You are not renting our work.
Do we need a technical team?
No. That is our core market. You bring the operational knowledge, we bring the technical layer.

We define the metric before we start: FTEs eliminated, hours saved, or revenue captured. At the end you get a one-page summary with before and after numbers.

Scoped on the intro call. We define the outcome, the timeline and the deliverable before we start. No hourly billing, no open-ended retainers.
What size company do you work with?
Middle-market operators doing $5M to $500M in revenue, founder-owned, family-owned or PE-backed. Typically 20 to 500 employees where overhead is the primary drag on EBITDA.
