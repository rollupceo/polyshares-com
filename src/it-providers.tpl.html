QUESTIONS PARTNERS ASK

The awkward ones, answered.

Will you go around me and take my client?
No. The contract is yours, the invoice is yours, the relationship is yours. We are subcontracted delivery and we sign to that.
Why start with training instead of a project?
Because it costs you nothing to offer and it tells you exactly which clients are ready. Guessing at a first project is how these programs stall.
Do I need AI engineers on staff?
No. That is the point. You bring the client relationship and the operational context, we bring the engineers who ship.
What does it cost me to start?
Nothing up front. No platform fee, no minimums, no exclusivity. Run training with a handful of clients and see what comes back.
Whose brand is on it?
Yours. We show up as your AI team in the room, in the documentation, and in the handover. If you would rather co-brand, that works too.
Who owns what gets built?
The client owns the IP outright at the end of the engagement. No lock-in, nothing rented, which is what makes it an easy sell for you.
Which of my clients is this for?
Non-technical operators doing $5M to $500M: home services, field services, light industrial, healthcare, professional services. Anywhere overhead is the drag on EBITDA.
