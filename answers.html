The short version, if you are only going to read one thing.

Most businesses with real operating history hold an asset they have never priced. It is the working record of how the job actually got done.

The AI labs are paying for that material. What sells is not volume but visible reasoning, and what stops most deals is the company discovering it does not own the records it assumed it did.

Polyshares represents the seller. We price the asset, handle anonymization before anything leaves your systems, take it to buyers, and take our fee from the proceeds. Deals typically land between $100K and $1M paid to the company, papered within a week and funded in eight to twelve.
