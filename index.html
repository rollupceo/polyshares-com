Questions

Questions owners ask first.

Does Polyshares buy company data, or broker it?

We buy it. Polyshares is a data lab and the counterparty on the deal. We license the data directly from the company that produced it, pay for it ourselves, and supply the AI labs. Nothing is listed anywhere and there is no third party for the company to deal with.

What does it cost the company?

Nothing. There is no fee and no commission, because we are not working on your behalf. We are buying. Money moves in one direction, from us to you.

How much does Polyshares pay?

Deals typically land between $100K and $2M paid to the company. Depth of operating history and how much visible reasoning the record contains move the number far more than raw volume does.

When does the money arrive?

Net 30 to 60, once the data has been approved, shared, and anonymized. We do not pay for a record we have not seen, so the review comes first.

Which companies qualify?

Any business with real operating history. Venture backed, middle market, or founder owned. The length and texture of the record matter more than headcount or revenue.

What happens to the data afterwards?

We anonymize it, build it into data sets, and supply AI labs. Use is bounded by the license, and we do not disclose which labs take which data.

What does Polyshares buy in an acquisition?

Lower middle market businesses, held rather than flipped. The current focus is MSPs and IT providers. Owners deal with us directly, with no auction to sit through.
