What we buy

Data licensing

Most companies hold an asset they have never priced.

There is almost always value in a business the owner has not priced. A great deal of it now sits in the operating record, the working history a company produces simply by running.

The AI labs need that material and are paying for it. The market is roughly a year old, and most owners do not yet know it exists.

We price the asset and take it to the buyers. The company keeps its equity and its data.

Operational data means the working record of the business. Slack, Drive, email, spreadsheets, CRM, design files.

Deal size
Typically $100K to $1M, paid to the company.
Structure
A license to defined operational data. Non-dilutive, with no equity and no debt.
Who qualifies
Any company with real operating history. Venture backed, middle market, or founder owned. The history matters more than the size.
Our fee
Taken from the proceeds. If nothing sells, you owe us nothing.
Mandate
Terms agreed and papered within a week of the first conversation.
Payment
Eight to twelve weeks to money in the company account, on the buyer closing.
Buyers
We hold the buyer relationships and run the process. Anonymization is ours to handle, and it happens before anything leaves the company. You take no meetings.
Ownership
You keep the business and the underlying data. The license is defined and bounded.
