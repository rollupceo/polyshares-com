About Michael

Michael Yannacacos was born in Canada but graduated from Jenks High School in Oklahoma. He is familiar with the Tulsa Metro area, Edmond, all of Northeastern Oklahoma, as well as Saint Petersburg, Tampa Bay, Sarasota, and Lakewood Ranch, Florida.

Prior to joining Coldwell Banker REALTORS®, Michael was a self-employed entrepreneur who started profitable businesses in landscaping and in pharmaceutical deliveries servicing nursing homes and hospice care. A United States Veteran, Michael served as a National Guardsman and, after being honorably discharged, chose to pursue his current career in real estate.

He has now been in the real estate industry for more than fifteen years and received his Oklahoma licensure in 2012. His resume includes staging, property management, and remodeling. He serves as a member of the Commercial Committee and the Property Management Committee through GTAR here in his home state of Oklahoma.

Michael’s prior and extensive experience as a successful entrepreneur makes him the ideal agent for clients who appreciate tenacity, a motivated work ethic, strong attention to detail, and a problem-solving mindset. As a tenured agent, he believes in a client-based approach for buyers and sellers alike.

“I understand that buying or selling a home can be an emotional experience as well. I always strive to make the process as smooth and stress-free as possible. I take the time to listen carefully to my clients’ needs and work with them to develop a customized plan that meets their unique requirements.”

His thorough understanding of the housing market, staying current on the latest trends and developments, networking skills, and ability to negotiate effectively ensures his clients make informed decisions and receive the best possible value for their investment. Michael focuses on luxury residential properties, investment properties, property management, and raw land development.
