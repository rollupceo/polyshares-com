Broken Arrow

Explore Broken Arrow, OK Real Estate and Community

Oklahoma's fourth-largest city, with a revitalized Rose District, a deep bench of new construction, and room to grow.

Broken Arrow has grown into a city in its own right rather than a Tulsa bedroom suburb. The Rose District downtown has become a genuine destination — restaurants, a performing arts center, and a farmers market that pulls from across the metro — while the manufacturing and aerospace employment base keeps jobs local.

For buyers, Broken Arrow offers the widest range in the metro: established mid-century neighborhoods, mature 1990s and 2000s subdivisions, and a steady pipeline of new construction on the south and east edges. It is where a lot of move-up buyers find the square footage they were looking for.
